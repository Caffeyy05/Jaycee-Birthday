Primary fixes verified:
- Supabase missing-table/schema-cache errors no longer reject/destroy a gift.

The container's managed Chromium policy sets URLBlocklist=['*'] and AudioCaptureAllowed=false.
That policy prevents this environment from performing a genuine browser navigation/microphone hardware test.
For that reason, V23 includes SYSTEM-CHECK.html, which performs the real environment tests in your own browser.
